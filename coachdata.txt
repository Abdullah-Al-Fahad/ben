BIO
Ben, a Chris Haueter Black Belt opened the Warrior Fitness Center in 2011 while still actively serving in the U.S. Army. His accomplishments are notable, with a decorated career as a professional fighter, an All Army Combatives medalist, and a black belt No-Gi Pan American medalist and a multi-time World, National, and Pan American medalist.

His background extends beyond the mat as well; he is a combat veteran of the U.S. Army, serving as a 13F. His service includes multiple deployments to the Middle East, during one of which he earned a Bronze Star for his

actions.

Stationed in Germany, Ben began his BJJ and MMA training and discovered his passion for teaching. In 2010, he had become the NCOIC and Senior Instructor of the Fort Carson Army Combatives Program,

Now, Ben divides his time between teaching developing athletes at Warrior Fitness Center and works as a Senior Economist for EcoMetricx where he utilizes his PhD in Economics and Public Choice.

BIO
Natalie Salcedo is a multi-talented martial artist with an impressive resume across Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and MMA. She is the reigning 105 lb champion at Peak Fighting Championship . She has has earned podium finishes in multiple prestigious jiu-jitsu tournaments including the IBJJF No Gi Worlds, IBJJF American Nationals, IBJJF No Gi Pan Ams, ADCC Orange County Open, and more.

Her Muay Thai career highlights include being a Thai Boxing Association World Champion, IFMA Muay Thai World Team Member (USA), Revgear North American Muay Thai Champion, and Colorado State Muay Thai Champion.

Originally from a military community, Natalie found her home in Colorado Springs after moving from Manhattan, KS. Her martial arts journey began in 2007, initially as a means of fitness, but truly flourished when she immersed herself in the sport and lifestyle in 2015.

Through martial arts, Natalie has learned goal-setting, accountability, and perseverance, skills that have fueled her success as an athlete and coach. She hopes to inspire others to believe in themselves and pursue their ambitions fearlessly. Outside the gym, Natalie holds a master’s degree in sports psychology and enjoys spending time with her husband, their pack of dogs, and exploring new restaurants.
